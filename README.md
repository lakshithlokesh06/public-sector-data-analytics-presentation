# Public Sector Data Analytics — AI Ethics Case Study 6

A 10-slide academic presentation based on the **Public Sector Data Analytics — Case Study 6** from the Princeton AI Ethics case-study material.

The presentation explores how data analytics, social network analysis, artificial intelligence, and machine learning were proposed to help reduce crime in the fictional city of New Leviathan, along with the ethical concerns surrounding the system.

## About the Case Study

The case follows:

- **New Leviathan** — a city facing economic decline, rising violent crime, racial tensions, and budget pressure.
- **Mayor Thalia Hobbes** — wants to reduce crime using limited public resources.
- **Wales Consulting Group (WCG)** — proposes a data-driven approach through its Public-Sector Division.
- **AI and Data Analytics System** — uses existing city records, social network analysis, AI, and machine learning to identify patterns and people potentially at risk of crime involvement.

The project initially focused on identifying potential victims and recommending social support services. It was later expanded toward identifying potential perpetrators and involving law enforcement, creating significant ethical concerns.

## Key Data Sources Mentioned in the Case

These sources are analysed to identify relationships, patterns, and potential risks.

## Ethical Issues

The case raises several important questions about the use of AI in public-sector decision-making:

- **Democracy** — Were citizens and the wider public given enough opportunity to participate?
- **Privacy and Secrecy** — Should citizens know how their data is being used?
- **Inequality** — Could biased historical data lead to disproportionate targeting of certain communities?
- **Fallibility** — Can predictions be mistaken for facts?
- **Determinism and Autonomy** — Could a prediction about someone's future behaviour affect how that person is treated?
- **Public Safety vs Civil Liberties** — How should governments balance crime prevention with individual rights?
